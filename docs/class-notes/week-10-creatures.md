### Building an Evolutionary Solver for procedural creatures

#### Genotype
The encoded instructions that define how to construct a creature.
#### Phenotype
The actual creature produced from those instructions, including its anatomy, physical properties, and behavior.
#### Fitness
A measure of how successfully the creature performs within an environment or selection process.
