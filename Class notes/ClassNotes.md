The concept of my world
- **A City Built Inside a Giant Tree**  
One impossibly large tree contains an entire world. Roots form underground cities, the trunk contains industrial districts, branches hold villages, and the highest leaves reach into a completely different climate.

- **The World That Changes With Emotion**  
The landscape reacts to the emotional state of its inhabitants. Calm regions become smooth grasslands, fear creates sharp mountains and fog, happiness creates colorful vegetation, and conflict physically fractures the terrain.
